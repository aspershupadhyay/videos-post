## Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
> Apple says the new MacBook Pro is "13× faster at A.I."
>
> Pause the ad on its last frame: "Performance testing against specific MacBook Pro systems with M1." That's a 2020 laptop.
> Against last year's M5, Apple's own number is 1.3×.
>
> And the 13× is "time to first token": how fast the A.I. *starts* answering, not the whole answer.
> (The "11× faster graphics" is the same trick: Blender ray tracing vs an M1, a chip with no hardware ray tracing.)
>
> Still on an M1? Real upgrade. Bought an M5 last year? Keep your money.

**Caption (Instagram / Shorts):**

In 2010 Steve Jobs said a touchscreen Mac doesn't work: "your arm wants to fall off." This week Apple shipped one.

Apple's answer to his complaint is that you never have to touch it. The trackpad and keyboard stay, the touch sensor is built into the
OLED panel, and a new hinge keeps the screen steady when you tap. We read the whole ad looking for the catch. The only fine
print is "Weight varies by configuration and manufacturing process."

MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display: from $2,899, out November 6.

Would you actually touch your laptop screen? 👇

# CatchTheError — brand for video

Taken from the CatchTheError carousel canvas (Case 017). A case-file look: paper, ink, a red pen
and rubber stamps. Every visual is evidence being examined.

## Do

- Paper `#F2E8D5` with 18–22% grain is home. Teal, rust and mustard are whole-frame worlds, never gradients.
- Display type is Bricolage Grotesque 800 at 75% width, uppercase, tight leading.
- Rust is the accent: one word or one stamp per frame. Mustard is the highlighter.
- Cards carry a 4 px ink border and a hard 10 px ink offset shadow, no blur.
- Motion comes from the medium: stamps slam (scale 1.4 → 1, 0.12 s, no bounce), red pen strikes draw
  left to right, highlighter sweeps grow behind words, paper slides in with weight and blur.
- Sources go on screen in mono, 28 px minimum.

## Don't

- No glow, neon, gradients, glass, particles or a dark void.
- No floating/breathing loops; a landed title is still.
- No logo docked in a corner; the mark lands once, on the end card.
- Never show a number the sources do not give.
